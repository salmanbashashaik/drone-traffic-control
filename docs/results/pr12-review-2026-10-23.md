## Blocking

### B1. A growing cluster gets contradictory latches
`_update_latches`, `filter.py:399–447`.

**The mechanism.** The colouring of a 3+ cluster is computed from scratch. It ignores the frames already latched in that cluster.
- A pair that latched while it was a two-drone conflict keeps turn-right (`ẑ × n`).
- Turn-right always pushes the middle drone in opposite directions towards neighbours on opposite sides.
- So the property in P2-SA-02 §5.1 and `INTERFACES.md` ("a drone squeezed between two neighbours gets opposite latches towards them") is false for any cluster that forms in stages.

**Shown directly** (`latch_dump.py`; b hovering, a at −30 m at 10 m/s, c at +50 m at −10 m/s):
```
head: step 0: latch ('a','b'): a sideways [0,-1,0], b sideways [0, 1,0]
head: step 5: latch ('a','c'): a sideways [0, 1,0], c sideways [0,-1,0]
head: step 9: latch ('b','c'): b sideways [0,-1,0], c sideways [0, 1,0]
head: step 35: b accel [-2.56,-0.53,0.52]      v2: b accel [-0.87,-0.86,0.03]
```
- At head, b is latched +y by `(a,b)` and −y by `(b,c)`; a is −y by `(a,b)` and +y by `(a,c)`.
- In v2 every latch is consistent: a +y, b −y, c +y.

**Measured cost.** No run entered d_eff, but margin is lost:

| Probe | head | v2 | pre |
|---|---|---|---|
| `staged.py`, c 2 s late | 5.650 m | 6.407 m | 6.392 m |
| `staged_sweep.py`, 128 cases, worst | 5.585 m | 5.759 m | 5.717 m |
| `staged_fuzz.py`, 567 random staged 3-drone runs, worst | 5.539 m | 5.539 m | 5.614 m |

- In the random staged fuzz head equals v2, so colouring that ignores existing latches is a flaw in both. Turn-right makes it hit every time a head-on pair is joined by a third drone.
- **Fix options:**
  - Seed `_two_colour` with the directions already latched in the cluster, and re-orient the turn-right latches when the cluster reaches 3.
  - Or, at minimum, correct the docs and add a staged-sandwich test.

## Non-blocking

1. **Turn-right is mostly decided by noise.** The rule applies only when the predicted miss is below `_MISS_CLEAR_M = 0.5 m`. At σ 1.5 m the relative position noise is about 2.1 m per axis, so the latched side comes from noise read once at entry.
   - `rightrule.py`, true head-on: 200/200, 153/200 and 118/200 latches turn right at σ 0, 0.5 and 1.5 m.
   - With a 1 m true miss at σ 1.5 m, 79/200 pairs latch against the true miss and hold it for the whole conflict.
   - Closed loop (`noisy_miss.py`, 40 seeds) is still far better than pre:
     - σ 1.5 m, true miss 2 m: head and v2 worst 6.021 m, 0/40 below d_eff; pre 2.946 m, 32/40 below.
     - σ 1.5 m, true miss 1 m: head 5.835 m, v2 5.936 m, pre 19/40 below.
   - So this is not a safety regression, but the docs should not present it as the aviation convention.

   - Add a unit test for: two drones turning right, a 3+ cluster coloured, and a staged cluster growing (see B1).
