## Range gate

`_exact_range_gate(config)` is the separation beyond which no row of a pair can bind for any state with speeds ≤ v_max. It is the largest of three bounds, one per row type; derivations are in its docstring and in `docs/architecture/PROPOSAL-scale-qp.md`:

| Row type | Gate at the defaults |
| --- | --- |
| backup (dominates) | 595.6 m |
| escape | 260 m |
| braking, including its tie-break | 167 m |

It grows with `lag_s` (611.9 m at 0.3 s) and shrinks with gamma (358.8 m at 10).

- **What it skips:** pairs beyond the gate skip the rollout and the QP. `_update_latches` still sees every pair, so latching is unchanged.
- **Test:** pairs just beyond the gate, closing at up to 2 v_max, have rows that hold over the whole box, for four configs.
- **Diagnostics change:** `n_pairs_in_qp`, `backup_rollout_steps`, `backup_rollout_cap_pairs` and the episode `p99_backup_rollout_steps` no longer count pairs beyond the gate.
- **Effect:** in the bench it only acts on city traffic at N ≥ 64 (rollout 36 → 26 ms at N = 64 city).

**Out of scope (founder decision): one converging cluster of all 64 drones.** (Since 51d3527 the solves have deadlines and this case is bounded, worst step 134 ms; see `deadline-2026-10-24.md`. The text below is the state at 9ebc06e.) Its p95 is 6.4 s, with a 55 s worst step, against 2.0 s on main. The cluster's QP has more than 256 variables, so it goes to OSQP at eps 1e-9. The outcome is better than main (0 fallbacks against 24, min sep 3.74 against 0.91 m), but no step bound holds there. A cap on solve time with a defined fallback would be a safety-behaviour decision, so it is listed as an open point, not built.

Where the time goes at N = 64 dense (mean ms per step): rollout 38.9 on 4 threads, everything else 16.4 (pair loop, `_independent_problems`, contract objects), `_update_latches` 2.8, build 2.6, solve 0.5. The rollout is now the largest part. Skipping more rollouts needs a tighter, state-dependent bound than the 596 m gate (not attempted).
