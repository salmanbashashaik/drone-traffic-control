## 2026-10-23 (research lane: consistent backup, P3-SA-02)

Research only: nothing here changes `src/utm_services/safety/`, `src/utm_services/simulation/` or `tests/`. Decided by Claude under the Decision authority rules unless marked otherwise. The acceptance bar for "adopt" and the protocol requirement in P3-SA-02 §5 were set by the founder on 2026-10-23.

- **Prototype location and contract.** `research/consistent_backup/cbk.py` keeps the signature and return types of `apply_filter`, with `latch` required, so the same runners drive main and CBK. The extra diagnostics go in an attribute `cbk`.
- **Library constants.** 53 candidates (brake, plus 26 world-frame sign vectors flown for 10 or 20 steps at 3.5 m/s² per axis), a 3 m clipping band for the selection score and a 0.5 m switching band. These follow TBC's time-varying backup with a fixed manoeuvre time.
- **Harness checked against main's baselines before use.** `harness.py` reproduces main's values: hnoise σ 0 7.241 m and sandwich mid 3.740 m (`docs/results/pr12-final-report.md`), hard seeds 1177, 4828 and 3810 at 4.765, 5.369 and 3.568 m (`docs/results/pr12-b1-2026-10-23.jsonl`).
- **Batched margins.** The pair margins are one batched matrix product per pair instead of a loop over steps. Same results; CBK latency at 6 drones fell from 214 ms to 118 ms on the shared CPU.
- **Exact prune.** A pair whose drones cannot come within `d_eff + 3 m` under any option gets the constant clipped margin without computing it (P3-SA-02 §4). The selection is unchanged by construction. It was added after the evaluation workers had started, so the evaluation ran without it.
- **Lite library (`cbk-lite`).** 11 candidates (brake, 8 horizontal directions, up, down; 20 steps only), measured as the pruned option the founder asked for if CBK misses 100 ms at N = 16.
- **OSQP retry at rho 10.** The evaluation had 4 CBK fallbacks, all OSQP non-convergence ("solved inaccurate", then the iteration limit) at rho 0.1. `cbk._solve` now retries at rho 10, warm-started at the backup action, after the two existing attempts. It acts only where both attempts fail, so only the 4 affected runs were re-run (`results/rerun_fallbacks.txt`): 0 fallbacks, same minimum separation.
- **Evaluation result against the bar (P3-SA-02 §6.2).** Item 1 fails in 15 cases (CBK lower than main by 0.12–1.29 m, 14 of them still above d_eff); item 2 fails on bent 10° and 20°; item 3 holds after the retry. Safety regression for the founder: under vnoise σ = 1.5, 2 of 20 CBK runs enter d_eff (5.183 and 5.094 m); main keeps all 20 outside. CBK reversals under noise are higher than main's (σ 1.5: 16 against 1 vertical, 10 against 0 horizontal).
- **Barrier term clamped in CBK rows (for the founder).** CBK's rows use `max(d − d_eff, 0)`; main's use `d* − d_eff` unclamped. With the clamp, an uncertified joint backup is only required not to get worse; main asks for recovery. This is a design choice of the prototype, recorded in P3-SA-02 §2.4 and listed as an open question; it changes nothing in main.
- **Adversarial review of the research branch (fresh subagent, read-only, no runs).** No blocker. Fixed in the docs: the slack cause in §2.4, the hysteresis claim in §2.3 (the band also applies when the held candidate is uncertified, unlike TBC Algorithm 2), the noise-reversal claim in §2.5, the §3 claims for bent 10° and 20°, the reversal reading for zero-nominal cases, the selection bias of the hard seeds, and the provenance above. Not changed in code, because each change would need a new evaluation: the hysteresis rule and saving `min_margin_m`.
- **`scale.py` capped (founder's instruction).** Each configuration (N = 6, 16, 64 × main, cbk-lite, CBK) starts from a dense mid-encounter state, flies 5 warm-up filter steps and then 20 timed closed-loop steps with zero nominal, in its own child process with a hard limit of 10 minutes. A configuration that hits the limit is reported as "> limit" with the steps it completed. Configurations run one at a time (parent plus one child), so the timings are not contended by our own runs.
- **Dense start state.** A random crowd aimed at one point, advanced in free flight to the time (0–6 s) with the most pairs within 20 m while no pair is closer than `d_eff + 1` m. The aim-point spreads (10, 10 and 40 m for N = 6, 16 and 64) are the densest that still give such a state for seed 0; at 20 m spread, N = 64 has no crowd with 10 m initial gaps.
- **Harness id order fixed.** `kinematic` mapped the filter's output to drones by sorting the agent ids as strings, which puts `d10` before `d2`. That misassigns actions for N ≥ 11. Every §6.2 run has N ≤ 6, so no §6.2 result changes. Actions are now looked up by id. `env_episode` now uses the active ids explicitly, which is the same order as before.
- **Decentralised cost is an estimate, not a measurement.** `scale.py` times the per-drone pieces at the N = 16 state: the drone's own option and finite-difference rollouts, one pair's margin matrix, and a one-drone QP with 5k rows. Per-drone cost = rollouts + k × pair margin + QP, for an assumed k = 6 and k = 15 neighbours in range. Selection scans, messaging and the sequential order of CSA Algorithm 1 are not included.
- **Latency result (P3-SA-02 §6.3), idle machine.** p50 / p95 at N = 16: main 53 / 57 ms, `cbk-lite` 66 / 77 ms, CBK 273 / 296 ms; at N = 64: main 366 / 388 ms, `cbk-lite` 1290 / 1664 ms, CBK 4276 / 4776 ms. Full CBK misses 100 ms at N = 16 and N = 64 and fails bar item 4 (5.2× main). `cbk-lite` holds item 4 (1.25×). For the founder: in the N = 16 timing state, `cbk-lite` reached 4.636 m against main's 5.198 m; the state was chosen for density, not safety, and all three filters end inside `d_eff` there.
- **Recommendation: adapt, do not adopt (P3-SA-02 §8).** Bar items 1, 2 and 4 fail, item 3 holds, with one safety regression. Kept for the founder's review; nothing in `src/` changes.
