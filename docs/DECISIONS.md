## 2026-10-23 (MARL training loop, P2-SA-01, Salman)

Decided by Claude under the Decision authority rules (training tooling; no filter code, threshold, contract or spec changed). Details: `docs/training/MARL_TRAINING.md`, `docs/formulation/P1-SA-01-method.md` §9.

- **Custom MAPPO in `training/`, not CleanRL.** `DroneEnv` already has the step contract; env step is about 0.1 ms; torch is an optional `train` extra pinned to 2.5.1. The runtime (`policy/learned.py`) is numpy-only.
- **Behaviour-cloning warm start from the reference, then PPO.** Recorded in artifact metadata. The learned policy therefore starts as the reference controller; PPO then changes it. A from-scratch run was not made, so the contribution of the warm start is unmeasured.
- **Observation preprocessing v3** (neighbour range gate at 250 m, arcsinh goal scaling). Each change came from a diagnosed failure on development seeds: `head-on-40` never completed (inputs saturated by neighbours up to 750 m apart) and one held-out drone stalled 2.8 m from its goal (input 0.003). The neighbour gate is inside the policy; the safety filter is not gated and still sees every drone.
- **Seed ranges.** Because those failures were found on seeds 9,000,000+ and on the shipped scenarios, those are development evidence only. The frozen T1 design was then evaluated on seeds 12,000,000+. Those seeds were consulted again for the T2-nominal policy, for the T1 policy under T2 profiles, and for the first shield-in-loop run, so they are a repeatedly used test set, not a single look (the first version of this entry said "once"; the review of PR #20 corrected that). The doc quotes fresh seeds first.
- **Checkpoint selection on 60 validation episodes, ties to the later checkpoint.** The first version used 24 episodes and a strict `>`, which kept iteration 20 of an under-trained run when validation saturated at 100. That run was discarded and retrained.
- **Two artifacts shipped:** T1-trained and T2-nominal-trained. The T1 policy lost completion under T2 disturbances (75–84% on the same 50 fresh seeds); the T2-nominal policy completes 100% under ideal and under the harsher realistic profile. The T2 profile parameters are the placeholders in `simulation/env.py`.
- **Shield in the training loop: no clear difference, not shipped.** The first 150-iteration run was not a fair comparison and is dropped. A full 300-iteration run was evaluated on a new seed set (15,000,000+): same violation pair-steps unshielded (8 vs 8), 0 shielded for both, 213 vs 204 interventions. One seed each; kept under `artifacts/ablations/` for traceability.
- **Not decided, for the founder:** whether `unshielded_marl` and `shielded_marl` in `utm-run` should load a trained artifact (`utm-run` still uses the reference as a labelled stand-in; `utm-replay` loads artifacts). Left unchanged because `evaluation/cli.py` is shared.
- **Loader hardening after the PR #20 review.** `obs_normalization_fingerprint` (a hash of the numeric constants, not the prose) is stored and checked, `weights_file` must be `actor.npz`, and the T2 artifact's `limitations` text now matches its training profile. The two shipped `metadata.json` files were updated in place; the weight hashes and artifact ids did not change, and the policy outputs on 500 random observations were bit-identical before and after.
- **Reviewed and left as is:** behaviour-cloning labels use the stateless PD law, not the PID reference; the T2 run's warm start uses ideal dynamics; dropped commands are stored as executed; no tests were added (standing instruction not to add tests unprompted). Listed in `MARL_TRAINING.md` known gaps.
- **Open limitation found on the 15,000,000+ seeds:** the T1 policy stalled one agent 2.04 m from its goal (goal radius 2.0 m). Not fixed (needs a retrain and a new unseen seed set); listed in `MARL_TRAINING.md`.
- **`utm-replay --pybullet --gui` retries `getContactPoints` and raises if it keeps returning `None`.** It lives in `training/replay.py` as a subclass, so the shared `simulation/pybullet_env.py` is unchanged. A silent skip would hide collisions, so it fails loudly.
- **Buildings in replays are scenery, not obstacles (founder asked for buildings, 2026-10-23).** `training/city.py` keeps every building at least 35 m from each drone's straight route; `DroneEnv`, the policy and the filter are unchanged and obstacle-blind. The clearance of flown tracks is measured (`city-clearance` result files), not claimed: 21.8 m minimum over 50 scenarios, 10.6 m in one head-on run. Obstacle-aware control (a static-box barrier in the filter, SANDO-style corridors) needs a contract change and is left to the founder with Anas and Aman.
- **Interactive 3D viewer replaces the plotly page.** Self-contained HTML with vendored three.js r128 (MIT); verified in headless Chrome. The PyBullet window stays available (`--pybullet --gui`) but is slow and was not usable for the founder, so the browser viewer is the supported way to look around the scene.
- **Six-drone suite on fresh seeds (18,000,000+), founder asked for 6 drones.** `evaluate.py --suite six`. Both policies complete 100% with 6 drones although trained on 2-4; unshielded violation pair-steps 13 (T1) and 6 (T2) against 61 for the reference; shielded 0 for both. The showcase seed (18000044) is the worst unshielded case of the 50 and is labelled as such. The seeds were consulted once for the table and once to choose the showcase.
- **Mirrored head-on scenario (`swap`), founder's design (2026-10-23).** d0's start is d1's goal and the reverse, 20-30 m apart, plus 4 random drones; implemented as `swap_scenario` (`scenarios.py`), `swap:` in `utm-replay` and `--suite swap`. An earlier "twin drones starting a few metres apart" idea was dropped and not reported. Probe seeds (24,900,000+) at gaps 20/25/30 m showed the unshielded pair collided in 20/20 episodes and the shielded pair stayed above 5.75 m; the reported fresh-seed run is 50 episodes at 25 m on seeds 24,000,000+: unshielded 344 violation pair-steps in 50/50 episodes, shielded 0.
